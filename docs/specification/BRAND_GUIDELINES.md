## Non-negotiable rules

- The approved source has a neon geometric monogram and the STANICODE wordmark, with a dark background, electric cyan, blue and purple.
- Integrate the letters **G, D, C** subtly in the geometric monogram. Do not display or explain full names. Do not include any surname in branding, UI, documentation or metadata.
- No ® symbol; no registration is established.
- Preserve the approved visual direction; create scalable SVG source, ICO and icons in multiple sizes, light-mode variant, splash and installer artwork.
- Use accessible contrast and maintain readability at small sizes.
- Prefer private repositories and avoid embedded personal details in binary metadata.
